KONTUR Backups

A private backup integration operated by the application administrator. It stores encrypted recovery archives in an authorized Google Drive account and checks that uploaded copies can be retrieved.

Google Drive access

The integration requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It can create backup files and manage files created by or explicitly authorized for this integration. It does not request general access to the account’s other Drive files, Gmail, contacts, or calendars.

Private operation

This is an internal administrative tool, not a public backup service. Google authorization is performed by the storage account holder. This public information page contains no backup files, credentials, application data, or server access details.

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use before authorizing the integration.

Privacy Policy

This policy covers the KONTUR Backups integration with Google Drive.

Access and use

The integration uses the Google Drive drive.file permission to upload, list, download, verify, and manage its authorized encrypted backup files and folders. File names, sizes, checksums, identifiers, and timestamps are processed to check backup completeness and apply the administrator’s retention settings. The Drive account’s internal permission identifier may be compared during migration to prevent switching backups to the wrong account.

Storage and protection

Backup contents are encrypted before upload. Application decryption keys are not uploaded by this integration. OAuth credentials are stored privately on the administrator-controlled system and used to renew access without repeated sign-in.

Sharing and limited use

Google Drive stores the encrypted files as the selected storage provider. Google user data is used only to operate and protect the backup and recovery functionality. It is not sold, used for advertising, used to train general-purpose AI models, or disclosed to unrelated parties. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Backups are retained according to the administrator’s configured retention policy. The account holder can revoke the integration through Google Account permissions. Revocation stops future API access; it does not itself delete previously uploaded files. The account holder can review and delete those files in Google Drive. Deletion may affect the ability to recover data.

This information site

This site includes no analytics scripts, advertising, sign-in forms, or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process ordinary web request information under its own privacy policy.

Contact

For access, privacy, or deletion requests, contact the application administrator through your existing private support channel. The Google authorization screen also identifies the configured support contact.

Terms of Use

KONTUR Backups is an internal administrative integration for authorized backup and recovery operations. It is not offered as a public storage service.

Authorized use

Authorize the integration only for a Google Drive account and data you are permitted to manage. Google services remain subject to the terms applicable to your Google account.

Recovery responsibility

The administrator is responsible for storage capacity, retention, access controls, preservation of decryption keys, and recovery testing. No storage provider or backup process guarantees uninterrupted service or recovery in every circumstance.

Stopping use

The storage account holder can revoke authorization through Google Account permissions. Existing backup files must be managed separately in Google Drive. Contact the application administrator through the existing private support channel before deleting recovery material.
